## 3. Balanced 24-Cell Pilot Design

The pilot partitioned the sky into 12 half-open Galactic-longitude intervals `[0,30)`, `[30,60)`, ..., `[330,360)`, each split into north (`b > 20 degrees`) and south (`b < -20 degrees`). Cell order was longitude ascending, then north, then south.

For target `N = 55,000` and `K = 24`, the implemented allocation was:

```text
base = floor(55,000 / 24) = 2,291
remainder = 55,000 mod 24 = 16
quota[i] = 2,292 for i < 16; otherwise 2,291
```

Thus the exact allocation was **16 cells × 2,292 plus 8 cells × 2,291 = 55,000**. An earlier verbal summary of “12 × 2,292 plus 12 × 2,291” was a reporting error: that expression totals only 54,996. The manifest, code, and arithmetic above are authoritative.

## 4. Why This Pilot Cannot Ship

The frozen v0.1 population definition states that `C_IR` is **all Gaia DR3 sources satisfying** the registered gates and that the population is built by a **single Gaia archive TAP query**. The pilot instead:

1. selected a fixed 55,000-row quota sample rather than all eligible sources;
2. imposed a new 24-cell balanced sampling plan;
3. used 120 keyset-paginated joined queries rather than the frozen single-query population construction; and
4. required non-null, bounded `teff_gspphot` at acquisition so every retained row could be stratified, although the frozen quiet-negative population list did not explicitly register that eligibility gate.

Those are material population changes. They were implemented and observed after the v0.1 and v0.1.2 freezes. They may be scientifically defensible, operationally necessary, and useful for avoiding sky-concentration bias, but they cannot be legalized retroactively by a failure record, implementation test, trusted-registry entry, or successful row-count gate.

The pilot also added or tightened the following row-level rules after the scientific freeze:

- it used AllWISE best-neighbour `angular_distance <= 1.5` arcseconds, while frozen v0.1 Section 6 said strictly `< 1.5` arcseconds;
- it required `duplicated_source = false`, although duplicated-source rejection was not listed in the frozen quiet-negative definition;
- it required finite/non-null `phot_bp_rp_excess_factor` and `teff_gspphot` at acquisition;
- it required AllWISE W1/W2/W3 magnitude uncertainties to be positive, below the uncertainty ceilings implied by the SNR thresholds, and exactly consistent with stored derived SNR within numeric tolerances;
- it accepted only absent or exactly `0000` `cc_flags`, which also excludes a W4 contamination flag even though frozen Section 6 named W1/W2/W3 only;
- it required absent or zero `ext_flg`, converting a frozen confounder signal into an acquisition exclusion;
- it required non-empty AllWISE and 2MASS designations, finite Ks, and strictly positive Ks uncertainty;
- it required the Gaia-hosted 2MASS best-neighbour distance to be `<= 1.5` arcseconds and enforced one retained joined row; and
- it introduced canonical W4 normalization: well-formed missing/partial or valid sub-threshold W4 became an all-null triple, while malformed present values failed closed.

These rules are reasonable candidates for a prospective registration, but their pilot application was not prospectively authorized. v0.1.3 registers them explicitly rather than pretending they were already frozen.

The pilot's `response_sha256` fields were decoded raw-response semantic hashes over the in-memory table's ordered columns, dtypes, and pandas row hashes. They were **semantic frame hashes**, despite the field name and despite being computed before W4/SIMBAD normalization; they were not hashes of exact HTTP response bytes, VOTable bytes, or a separately retained raw-response file. The pilot therefore cannot reconstruct each remote response independently from the manifest. v0.1.3 distinguishes transport bytes, decoded raw-table semantic identity, normalized-table semantic identity, and artifact byte identity.

The pilot temporarily established exact historical checkpoint and final-release allow-list entries in the working implementation. Those pilot-only allow-lists were removed from active code and the trust registry after the non-conformance determination. No current allow-list or loader trust path authorizes these bytes; their identities remain only in this failure record for quarantine and audit.

## 5. Findings Preserved From the Pilot

The pilot demonstrated the following implementation lessons without authorizing scientific use:

1. **Balanced acquisition is operationally viable.** Every cell reached its exact quota with bounded 500-row keyset pages.
2. **The final-size margin was adequate in this observation.** The BP/RP and registered external exclusions retained 54,014 controls, 4,014 above the minimum. This observed margin is not used to tune any v0.1.3 scientific threshold.
3. **`teff_gspphot` must be an explicit eligibility gate if temperature stratification is mandatory.** It cannot remain an implicit implementation assumption.
4. **Optional W4 requires a canonical missingness rule.** Catalog-missing, partial-but-well-formed, or complete sub-threshold W4 triples were normalized to wholly absent; malformed, non-finite, nonpositive-uncertainty, or internally inconsistent W4 values failed closed. W4 absence did not remove a source because W4 is optional.
5. **Raw and normalized exclusion counts differ legitimately.** SIMBAD relationship joins returned exact duplicate rows. Provenance must preserve the raw response identity and count while the release must expose the deterministic normalized result.
6. **Response hashes alone are insufficient for long-term reconstruction.** Every request needs contained raw-response and normalized-response evidence artifacts, not only a trace digest.
7. **Raw acquisition and final release can have different producers.** Their software identities must be recorded separately and bound by exact artifact, manifest, semantic-content, source-tree, and lock hashes.
8. **Artifact loaders must prove derivation, not merely schema.** The pilot loader re-ran Gaia/AllWISE/2MASS gates, BP/RP derivation, external-exclusion relationships, stratum counts, request ordering, and semantic hashes.
9. **Resource and path defenses are part of scientific integrity.** Oversized YAML/Parquet, aliases, traversal, symlinks, dirty source/config/lock trees, existing output directories, partial publication, and forged manifests must fail closed.
10. **Coarse cell balance is not a representativeness claim.** The population is suitable only for matched, coverage-stratified calibration within its registered coverage class.

The directory `controls/quiet_negative_v0.1.2_hardened/` and its raw checkpoint remain quarantined and uncommitted. Temporary pilot allow-list entries have been removed. Their hashes and aggregate findings may be cited only as a failed/pilot acquisition record.

If any downstream cache or local analysis has consumed the pilot, that result is exploratory and must be discarded. v0.1.3 requires deterministic reacquisition from cursor zero in every cell after its own pre-registration is frozen.
